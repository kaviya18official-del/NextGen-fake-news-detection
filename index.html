Examine conflicting ratings and publisher discrepancies without picking arbitrary winners.

Forensic Discrepancy Case Study: Transit Grant Allocations

Statement: "The state transportation department stated that infrastructure grant allocations increased by 40 percent under the regional transit agreement."

Publisher Rating / Verdict Review Date Core Finding
FactCheck.org Mostly False 2024-02-15 Calculation compares nominal funding without adjusting for inflationary inflation indexing.
State Auditor Report Accurate (Gross Basis) 2024-02-18 Gross cash disbursement table confirms 40% numerical increase in nominal state dollars.

⚠️ Evidence Verdict: EVIDENCE CONFLICT

Both organizations reviewed genuine documentation but arrived at diverging ratings due to differing baseline accounting methodologies. NextGen NewsVerify preserves both findings and highlights the conflict rather than arbitrarily favoring one publisher.
